# Real erosion of frozen nominal caps — research

**Sourcing:** gov.il/Tax-Authority PDFs & search engines were blocked; findings rest on kolzchut, Hebrew Wikipedia/Wikisource, and Israeli tax-professional pages (fetched 2026-08-30). Freeze status often proven by identical nominal figures year-over-year rather than an explicit statute cite → some **[verify]**.

## 1. Classification — two clusters
**Frozen (CPI-normal, but frozen 2024/25–2027):**
- **קצבה מזכה ceiling ₪9,430/mo** — flat across 2024–2026; legislated 2024–27 window + post-2027 CPI resumption **[verify]**.
- **Income-tax brackets** — indexed 2024, frozen 2025; 2026 partial/discretionary widening of middle bands only.
- **Surtax (מס יסף) ₪721,560/yr** — frozen 2025–2027 (חוק ההתייעלות … הקפאת עדכוני מס); +2% capital-income surtax added 2025.
- **Credit-point value ₪242/mo** — flat 2024–2026.

**Indexed up (model as ~constant real):** gemel-lehashkaa cap (₪81,711→₪83,641), average-wage pension base (₪13,769), keren-hishtalmut salary ceiling (~₪15,712, sticky), Bituach Leumi old-age allowance (CPI-indexed w/ negative-CPI protection).

## 2. Key finding — no catch-up after a freeze → permanent real erosion
Normal cadence: annual CPI indexation each 1 Jan (Ordinance Ch. ו׳1). **But after a freeze, indexation resumes from the frozen level — no catch-up.** Evidence: credit point held ₪242 across 2024–26 (no recovery jump); 2026 bracket widening was partial/discretionary, not mechanical CPI catch-up; 2014 freeze precedent had no catch-up. **However**, over the long run caps broadly tracked CPI *between* freezes (קצבה מזכה ₪8,190 (2012) → ₪9,430 (2024) ≈ +1.2%/yr nominal, with deflation-year dips). Net picture: **roughly real-constant on average, punctuated by freeze episodes that each carve a permanent real notch.**

## 3. Recommended real-erosion default (קצבה מזכה ceiling)
It's a legislative/political choice → an assumption with a range:
- **Near-term freeze (through ~2027):** flat nominal = **full-inflation erosion ~2.5–3%/yr** (near-certain, not an assumption).
- **Long-run default: ~1%/yr real erosion** (base); **0%/yr** low (faithful re-indexation); **~full inflation** high (never re-indexed).
- **Offsetting tailwind:** the §9א exemption *rate* on the ceiling rises **57%→67% by 2028** (תיקון 275) — partly counters the ceiling erosion.

## 4. Modeling recommendation — two knobs
- **Frozen cluster** (קצבה מזכה ceiling, brackets, credit point, surtax) → share **one "tax-ceiling real-erosion %/yr"** (default ~1%/yr long-run + explicit flat-nominal segment through 2027).
- **Indexed cluster** (gemel cap, avg-wage base, old-age allowance) → **~0% real erosion (constant real)**; lumping them with the frozen group would understate them.
- **Impact ranking for a retiree:** (1) **קצבה מזכה ceiling** — sets the tax-exempt pension slice, highest impact; (2) income-tax brackets (bracket-creep on the taxable annuity portion); (3) Bituach Leumi allowance — CPI-safe, model flat-real; (4) surtax — only bites >₪721,560/yr (HNW cases).
